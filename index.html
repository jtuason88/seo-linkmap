Section 05 · sequencing

How to ship it

Ordered by what unblocks the most downstream value. Phases 1 to 4 carry most of the impact and need almost no new copy — the anchor phrases are already on the pages.

Phase 1 · Week 125 links

Build the three missing hubs

Ship /staffing-roles, /industries and /locations. Nothing else compounds until 17 of the 21 priority pages have a real parent — and explicit hierarchy is what AI crawlers use to work out what you actually sell.

Phase 2 · Week 10 links

Make the BoothOS page readable

The BoothOS comparison table is an image and the role-page FAQs returned no text. No links here — just making content that already exists visible to crawlers.

Phase 3 · Week 216 links

Unlock the two dead-end solution pages

The AI operations page and BoothOS pass zero equity onward. The five industry sections on the AI page already contain the anchor copy — this is linking, not writing.

Phase 4 · Week 341 links

Link up the five industry pages

14 of the 45 role cards become links, plus the BoothAgent and BoothOS mentions already sitting in prose unlinked. Highest-volume, lowest-effort phase.

Phase 5 · Weeks 4-518 links

Rebuild the two location pages

Add a four-card roles module, a nearshore-vs-offshore comparison, real client proof and localised blog cards. Rewrite both H1s off the bare country name.

Phase 6 · Week 662 links

Lateral-link the role cluster, swap the boilerplate blogs

Sibling links on the four overlapping role pairs, plus topical blog cards replacing the identical trio on nine pages.

Phase 7 · Week 619 links

Feeders and the two healthy solution pages

Homepage and guide links into the new hubs, plus a light touch on the EOR and managed outsourcing pages — they already link well and don't need much.

Phase 8 · OngoingContent

Close the content gaps

Eight priority clusters have zero supporting posts: insurance, logistics, staffing & recruitment, customer service, IT services, software development, creative & design, data entry. Commission a healthcare and a Colombia case study.

Section 06 · beyond linking

Additional structural fixes for SEO and AI crawling

These sit alongside the link map rather than inside it. Two of the critical items aren't links at all — the BoothOS comparison table is an image and the role-page FAQs appear to be client-side rendered. For answer engines, content that isn't in the HTML doesn't exist, which makes those a bigger win than any single link above.

SeverityTypeFindingFix

Three things to watch while implementing

  • Global nav links don't count. Every sibling link on the role, industry and location pages currently lives only in the header dropdown. Because that markup is identical site-wide, it passes no topical signal. Every link in this map is a contextual one — that distinction is the whole point of the exercise.
  • Don't let /industries/staffing-recruitment cannibalise /solutions/talent-acquisition-eor. Both target hiring intent. Keep the industry page's anchor narrow — "back-office support for your own EOR and payroll obligations" — so it reads as serving staffing firms, not competing with them.
  • Add links as you touch pages, not all at once. 181 contextual links appearing site-wide overnight is a bigger pattern than the links themselves. Following the phases spreads it over six weeks, which is both safer and easier to attribute in your rankings data.
